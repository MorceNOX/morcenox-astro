• Overview:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This application is designed to be your astrological companion.
Here, you can calculate and visualize astrological charts, 
view the current transit chart, and access tables and panels to 
assist you in deep analysis and full horoscope interpretation.

You can zoom in and out the charts, pan them across the screen,
and use animation mode to visualize the past and future 
movements of planets and other celestial objects. 
You can even control the pace of the animation! 

Navigation is handled entirely by arrow keys, function keys,
menus and intuitive shortcuts — all within your terminal.

The modules provide charts, tables and panels, accessible 
via function and numerical keys, which contains all the data you 
need to make good interpretations and also predictions.

The texts are direct and objective, yet as complete as possible to 
guide you throughout the charts and tables. Color coding is also 
used everywhere to help you quickly interpret the significance of 
the data.

We also use colors within the chart to differentiate celestial 
bodies according to their types and mathematical points.

All calculations are performed in the light of Classical 
Astrology, though we also display the outer planets and their 
relationships with all other bodies in the chart and tables. 
Using these modern additions is entirely up to your preference.

Additionally, you can access settings to configure your charts 
according to various Classical Astrology systems. You can also 
set a default city to generate quick charts for your current 
location and time.

The app's core was designed to ensure accuracy in all 
calculations and offer the widest possible scope, making it an 
extremely useful astrological tool for any astrologer, from 
students to the most experienced professionals.   
 
The interface was designed to offer efficiency and beauty; 
most actions require only one or two keystrokes, and the 
color system displays relevant information across all modules.
 
To save a custom chart, simply enter the data and select the 
save option from the main menu.

To view and interact with the action modules hit the function 
or numeric keys of the keyboard while the chart wheel is being 
displayed, or the key [M] to open the action menu and choosing
the desired one. 

Some tables and panels have more options accessible while 
they are open.

Whenever you wish you can open this help screen and see 
immediately the keys and their respective table or 
panel, or go directly to the desired topic.

This application is developed in C, utilizing the ncurses 
library for visual elements and the Swiss Ephemeris for 
astronomical calculations. This ensures a fast, solid, and 
accurate system that is precise and visually appealing, 
even within a terminal environment.

All data is stored in an SQLite database provided by this
application.

We utilize an up-to-date free database of cities provided by
https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database, 
which we have enhanced with structural improvements using the 
pytz (timezonefinder) and srtm libraries to better serve the 
application's needs (These libraries were only used to enhance 
the application's database during the development phase. 
They are not used by the program and you don't need to install 
them).

The cities' database contains approximately 153 thousand
cities. It is good enough for a free and well-structured database,
working on a free astrological software, but small villages may 
not be listed. Therefore, for small villages, you should look for 
the name of the town where it is located, or a city very close 
to it. 

For example, Princess Diana was born in Sandringham, a small 
village located in King's Lynn borough, in the county of Norfolk.
You will see that Sandringham is not listed. So, to get her chart, 
you must select United Kingdom as the country, then look for 
King's Lynn in Norfolk county or state. (County or State is the 
2nd column in the cities table).

The same happens to the Friedrich Nietzsche's chart. He was born
in Röcken, a small village that was recently annexed to the town 
of Lützen, in Germany. So you must select Lützen, in the 
Saxony-Anhalt state, as the Nietzsche's hometown.

This software links with the ICU library (libicu), which is 
licensed under the ICU License and Unicode License. 
You can find the full license text at github.io 
(https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/main/LICENSE)

Astrological information has been compiled from various 
authoritative sources related to Classical Astrology and
some of them is included in the Technical Topics.

The application works 100% offline; no data is sent to or 
retrieved from the internet.

Available options in the Main Menu:


• Quick Chart: View chart for current time and location
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Displays the chart wheel for the current time (now) and the 
pre-set default location. The chart is animated and 
updates every second.

When hitting this option the previous loaded chart is replaced
by the quick new chart. If you didn't save it you've lost it.

Note: The loaded chart info is displayed at the bottom status bar.


• New Chart: Configure chart parameters
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Define the parameters for chart generation. This allows you 
to create Natal Charts or set data for specific historical 
events or projects, or for any other purpose.

The selection boxes appear in the following sequence:

1. Date (set year, month, and day using the arrow keys).

2. Time (set hour, minute, and second using arrow keys). This must
   be the local time, the time in effect in the birthday place and
   not the universal time.

3. The gender of the native; or neuter for non personal
   charts.

4. Country (Select from the scrolling list).

5. City (Select from the scrolling list; the State/County/Province
   is displayed in the 2nd column to help you distinguish 
   homonymous cities).

6. Timezone (TZ) (Displays the timezone from the cities 
   database; you may confirm or manually type a new one). Actually 
   this is shown only for reference, because the program allways
   calculates the correct timezone for the selected city.

7. DST (Daylight Saving Time: choose between Yes, No, or Auto. 
   If Auto, the application applies the correct DST 
   interval for the specific date).

8. Chart Name (Type a name for your new chart). The name of the 
   current chart will appear by default. You can maintain this
   name to overwrite the current chart with the new data or
   select another name to get a new chart.

The values are selected by using the arrow keys and <ENTER> to 
confirm.

You can hit the <ESC> key in any box to cancel the edition.

After setting the data, remember to save the chart. If you 
close the application without saving, or if you open the 
Quick Chart, the data will be lost.

The Quick Chart will overwrite the selected data by the 
current time and default location.


• Load Chart: Load a previously saved chart
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Scroll through a list of your saved charts and select one to load.
The chosen chart will become the "current chart".



• Save Chart: Save the current chart
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Save the current chart data to the database.

A dialog will ask you about the chart name, which you can 
preserve the current or set a new one.

This option can also be used to save the current loaded chart 
with a different name (so you can delete or not the previous one).

If you set an existing chat's name the previous one will be 
overwritten after the confirmation.



• Delete Chart: Delete a previously saved chart
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Select a chart from the list and hit <ENTER> to delete it. 
Note: This action is permanent and removes the chart from the
database.

It is very usefull to clean up the things.



• Display Chart: Show the full astrological chart
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Display the loaded chart even if it has not been saved yet.

The status bar in the main menu screen shows the data of the 
current loaded chart to be displayed.



• Settings: Modify default application settings
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This option opens a dialog to modify display modes and 
calculation parameters and other options.

You can set:

   • Display Mode (Dark/Light): Changes the color schemes 
     for the chart wheel and tables.
       
   • House Systems: Choose from various systems to suit 
     your needs:
    
    The available house systems and their classification:
    
        1) Natural or Simbolic Systems:
        W-Whole Signs, E-Equal Houses
        
        2) Space-based Non-quadrant System:
        M-Morinus
        
        3) Quadrant Systems:
        
            3.1) Ecliptic System:
            O-Porphyry
            
            3.2) Space-based Systems:
            C-Campanus, R-Regiomontanus
            
            3.3) Space and Time based System:
            B-Alcabitius, P-Placidus, K-Koch, T-Topocentric
        
    P.S.1: The system will automatically switch to O-Porphyry 
    if the selected latitude is above 66.5° or below -66.5° 
    when using B-Alcabitius, P-Placidus, or K-Koch.
    
    P.S.2: House numbers in the minor circle of the 
    chart wheel indicate houses boundaries.
    

   • Triplicity System: Choose between Morinus, Dorotheus, 
     and Ptolemy to determine the rulership of the elements.
    
   • Planetary Orbis: Modify the orbis value for all planets,
     luminaries and the lunar nodes used in aspect calculations.
     Note: We do not use "orbis by aspect"; each planet has a 
     fixed orbis applied to all its aspects.
    
   • Orbis for Parallel Aspects: Specific orbis values can 
     be set for parallel and contra-parallel aspects.
    
   • Modern Planet Exaltation: Although based on Classical 
     Astrology, you can choose whether to include the 
     exaltation rulerships of the outer planets. 
     (Note: We do not consider modern planets for domicile 
     rulership).
     
   • Show Modern Planets: You can choose if the 3 modern planets
     will be part or not of your charts. But remember that we 
     do not include dignities by domicile for these planets.
     
   • The Default Gender: Male, Female or Neuter.


All settings are persistent and saved in the application database.


• Change Default Location: 
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Set the default city for "Quick Charts".
 
The chosen city will update immediately and will persist 
upon restarting the application. 

This option does not overwrite previously saved chart and does not
change the city of the currently loaded chart, it only indicates 
to the application what is the city for the new Quick Chart.

   • Enter: Select current item
   • ESC: Exit menu and quit the application

• The Chart Wheel:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When a chart is loaded, the wheel is calculated according 
to the selected data.

The status bar in the Main Menu shows the location and time 
for the current chart. Here this information is displayed at the 
top left corner of the screen.

The chart screen also displays detailed information 
while showing the wheel and your Horoscope in the center.

The wheel is divided into 12 segments representing the signs. 
The 12 zodiac signs are displayed prominently using ASCII art.

The two main axes — Ascendant/Descendant and 
Midheaven/Imum Coeli — are also prominently displayed on the 
chart wheel.

Next to the outer circle, if you wish, you can see the rulers 
of the decans by pressing [D], three by sign, according to the 
Chaldean order, each one rulling ten degrees of the respective 
sign. The ruler charactere is drawn at the center of its decan.

You can also see the terms rulers by pressing [B]. Each term 
ruler is drawn in the beggining of its term. There are five
terms by sign and their degrees vary according to the chosen
system.

This way you can graphically see the rulers by term and decan of 
a body in the chart wheel.

Below the signs, planets and points rotate within their 
respective signs, displaying their angle (degree and 
minutes) and retrograde symbol (if applicable).

The chart displays a number in the inner and in the outer 
circles indicating the respective house cusp. So you can see the
boundaries of the houses.

In the center, in ASCII art, appear the symbols of the planets 
rulling respectively the day and the hour in effect at the 
date/time of the chart.

On the right side, you can see a table indicating the positions
of the planets and other celestial bodies.

You can also see the table of house cusp positions on the left 
side of the screen.

When zooming in on the chart, all information next to the wheel
is hidden if the second zoom level is active, except for the 
planet and house tables, which disappear at the third zoom level.

You can zoom in/out and pan the chart.
Use [+] or [-] to zoom and the arrow keys to pan.
The [R] key resets the chart wheel to its original size and 
position.

Type [H] to show the houses boundaries in the chart.

You can activate animation mode by pressing [A]. Use ] and [ 
to adjust the animation pace in 60-second increments. 
A positive pace shows future events; a negative pace simulates 
traveling back in time.

• The Colors:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

To differentiate celestial bodies, the chart uses color coding:

   • The 7 Main Planets (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, 
     Jupiter, and Saturn) are displayed in Blue.
    
   • The 3 Modern Planets (Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto) 
     are displayed in Magenta.
    
   • Points and Angles (Asc, Desc, IC, MC, Vertex, Part of 
     Fortune, Syzygy Ante Nativitatem, North/South Node) are 
     in Black.
    
The signs are color-coded by element:

   • Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius): Red
   • Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): Blue
   • Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): Yellow
   • Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn): Green

• The Animation:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

By default, "Quick Chart" is animated at a 1-second interval, 
allowing you to track celestial motion almost in real-time.

The animation updates the chart every second in the pace displayed
at the right bottom corner of the screen.

To animate a loaded chart, press [A]. Use ] to increase 
speed and [ to decrease speed by 1 minute per step. 
The clock and Moon phase in the upper corners will update 
accordingly to the animation pace, as well as the objects within
the chart wheel and the planetary rulers.

The wheel spins 1° clockwise every time the Ascendant adds 1°.

• The Strength of The Planets (F3 in the Dignities Table)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Here we show the score of each planet in their essential and 
accidental dignities, as well as the strength of each one 
according to the score and the weigh attributed by the planet's 
relevance.

The calculation is performed in three steps:

    1. Sum of the essential and accidental dignities, attributing
       a weigh of 70% for the essential and 30% for the 
       accidental dignities scores;
        
    2. Normalization of the weighted sum taking into account a
       minimum and a maximum reference values;
       
    3. Appliying the planetary weigh according to their 
       relevance and dividing by ten to get usefull and
       tangible values as the final strength.
       
So, we get relevant values to measure and evaluate a planet's
capacity.

Of course this calculation is only valid to have a glimpsed of the 
overall dignity or debility of a planet. It is an auxiliary tool. 
The use of the dignities tables and considering each dignity or 
debility is crucial to make a better and complete interpretation.

Use [↓↑] to scroll the table.

• Aspects (F3)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Displays aspects between the objects. Only the 5 main aspects 
are used: Conjunction (0°), Opposition (180°), Trine (120°), 
Square (90°), and Sextile (60°).

Except for Conjunctions and Exact Aspects, the program 
does not validate out-of-sign aspects.

We do not use "orbis by aspect." The orbis is always the 
average of the orbis of the two involved bodies. An aspect 
is valid if the distance between the 2 objects is within the 
calculated orbis.

The table shows the distance in degrees, allowing you to 
measure the strength of each aspect.

A letter 'a' or 's' is shown at the right side of the aspect 
symbol indicating if the aspect is applicative or
separative. If the aspect is exact (partile), no letter is 
displayed after the aspect symbol.

Color coding indicates the quality of the aspect: Blue (favorable:
trine and sextile), red (unfavorable: opposition and square), 
or magenta (specific factors: conjunction).

Note 1: You can control the planetary orbis in the "Settings" menu.
Note 2: Although they are not physical objects, tradition lectures 
recomend that the lunar nodes orbis be the same as the Moon orbis.

You can press F3 again to see the Declination Aspects (parallel &
contra-parallel).

Use [↓↑] to scroll the table.




• Parallel & Contra-Parallel - F3 in the Aspects Table
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Shows Parallel and Contra-parallel aspects, which are vital 
for estimating the strength of the relationship between bodies. 

The relationship between bodies is strengthened (for good or 
evil) by parallel or contra-parallel aspects. 

The planetary orbis is not considered here; a default 
orbis of 1.5° is set for both.

Color coding indicates the quality of the aspect: Blue (favorable:
parallel), or red (unfavorable: contra-parallel).

Note: The application consider the SAN's (pre-natal syzygy) 
declination as the Moon's declination as in the moment of the 
previous conjunction/opposition to the Sun.

This feature is available as an independent option when
selecting by the menu action.

Use [↓↑] to scroll the table.

If there is no aspect by sign between two objects, so they are in

This option is available as an independent option when
selecting by the menu action.

• Planetary Hours (F4)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Displays the planetary hours for the entire astrological day, 
the length of the hours, and their respective rulers.

Every planetary hour is 1/12 of the period (day or night) length 
which are calculated according to the times of sunrise and sunset,
considering that the planetary day starts on the sunrise and ends
on the next sunrise. The application takes this in account when
working with the planetary hours.

You will notice that after midnigth when the time in the chart 
indicates, for example, Wednesday, which is the real official
Wednesday, the ruler of the night will be the ruler of the 
Tuesday nigth, because the entire planetary day ends only at the 
next sunrise.

Press [i] in this screen to open the Planetary Hour Analysis
window.

• Hylegiacal and Figuris Almuten (F7)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Here we can see which planets are the lord of the chart and of the
hylegiacal points, as the Ascendant, the Sun, the Moon, the Part of
Fortune and the Syzygy Ante-Nativitatem (SAN).

The almuten is the planet which has more dignities in determined
longitude or point in the chart. 

The planet with more dignities in the hylegiacal points is the 
Almuten Figuris, or the Lord of the Chart.

The Almuten for a point is the planet with more essential
dignities in that location, also added points according to the 
house of the planet.

The Almuten Figuris is the planet with more dignities in all the 
five hylegiacal points, considering also points per house, for the 
regents of the day or night and the hour. 

If two or more planets received the same score, the criteria to 
elect the winner is:

1) the one which has more dignities in hylegiacal points, 
2) the one which rules more planets and points in the chart, and
3) the one which has more apects in general.

2. Traditional Preditctive Framework (Radix Confrontation)

The module prevents isolated, generic annual predictions by 
executing a traditional hierarchical cross-examination between 
the Solar Return and the Birth Chart (Radix) through seven 
distinct checkpoints:

     Identifies the planet commanding the annual chart based on 
     essential and accidental dignities computed for the return 
     moment. Its raw operational strength is filtered against 
     its root Natal Dignity Score, showing whether its annual 
     promises will manifest smoothly (strong radix) or through 
     heavy evolutionary hurdles (weak radix).
   
   • [CHECK II] Radix House Transit: Projects the ecliptic 
     coordinate of the annual Almuten back onto the native's birth 
     horizon via swe_house_pos(armc_natal, lat_natal, ...). 
     This maps exactly which natal life-sector (e.g., Career in 
     House 10, Finances in House 2) is being physically triggered 
     by the "Manager of the Year."
   
   • [CHECK III] Time-Lord Co-Alignment: Cross-checks the annual 
     Almuten against the Hellenistic Profection Lord of the Year. 
     A perfect match signals an extraordinary "Turning-Point Year," 
     giving the planet double cosmic authorization to trigger 
     inevitable, life-shaping events.
   
   • [CHECK IV] Firdaria Cronocrator Alignment: Embeds the annual 
     chart into the medieval long-term time stream. It verifies 
     if the Lord of the Year matches the current Firdaria Master 
     Ruler (Macro-cycle) or Sub-Ruler (Active sub-period), 
     validating if immediate daily events are backed by long-term 
     destiny trends.    
   
   • [CHECK V] Annual Ascendant Projection: Computes where the 
     annual local horizon cuts through the birth 
     houses. This reveals the primary target of the native's vital 
     energy and focus during these specific 12 months.
     
   • [CHECK VI] SOLAR RETURN ASCENDANT PROJECTION: Computes the 
     inverse horizon intersection by projecting the native's 
     radical Ascendant coordinate back onto the dynamic Solar 
     Return wheel. While Check V maps the objective environment 
     knocking at the native's door, this checkpoint uncovers the 
     psychological attitude, internal vitality, and subjective 
     coping mechanisms the native will adopt when facing the 
     year's challenges.
   
   • [CHECK VII] NATAL ASCENDANT PROJECTION IN SOLAR RETURN: 
     The Cross Projection: Executes a multi-layered combinatorial 
     analysis that bridges Check V (circumstantial reality) 
     and Check VI (psychological response). By contrasting the 
     external demands against the internal disposition, it detects 
     structural alignments or structural friction (such as a 
     public House 10 career spotlight colliding with an internal 
     House 12 urge for withdrawal), preventing flat, linear 
     interpretations.
