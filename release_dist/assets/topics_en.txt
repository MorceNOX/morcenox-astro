• House Systems - a brief explanation:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
        
        1) Natural or Simbolic Systems:
        
        • W - Whole Signs: A symbolic system where each house 
          corresponds to an entire zodiacal sign. The sign 
          containing the Ascendant becomes the 1st house, and the 
          house cusps are always at 0° of each subsequent sign.
        
        • E - Equal Houses: A regular system that uses the exact 
          degree of the Ascendant as the cusp of the 1st house. 
          Each subsequent house cusp is projected at exactly the 
          same degree in consecutive signs (30° intervals along 
          the ecliptic).
        
        
        2) Space-based Non-quadrant System:
        
        • M - Morinus: A universal, three-dimensional system 
          that divides the celestial equator into 12 equal 30° 
          segments starting from the meridian. These divisions 
          are projected onto the ecliptic via circles passing 
          through the ecliptic poles, ignoring the local 
          horizon and geographical latitude.
        
        
        3) Quadrant Systems:
        
            3.1) Ecliptic System:
            
            • O - Porphyry: A straightforward quadrant system 
              that calculates the exact longitude distance 
              between the Ascendant and the Midheaven (MC). 
              Each of the four quadrants is then divided 
              directly along the ecliptic into three houses 
              of equal zodiacal length.
            
            
            3.2) Space-based Systems:
            
            • C - Campanus: A three-dimensional system that 
              divides the local prime vertical (the great circle 
              passing through the East, West, and Zenith points) 
              into 12 equal segments, which are then projected 
              onto the ecliptic.
            
            • R - Regiomontanus: A three-dimensional system that 
              divides the celestial equator into 12 equal 
              segments (starting from the meridian) and projects 
              them onto the ecliptic using circles that intersect 
              at the North and South points of the local horizon.
            
            
            3.3) Space and Time-based Systems:
            
            • B - Alcabitius: A semi-arc system that calculates 
              the time it takes for the Ascendant to travel to 
              the Midheaven during diurnal rotation, dividing 
              that temporal arc into three equal parts to 
              determine the intermediate cusps.
            
            • P - Placidus: A dynamic system based on diurnal 
              and nocturnal semi-arcs. House cusps are determined 
              by the points where the celestial positions 
              intersect specific fractions (1/3 and 2/3) of a 
              planet's or star's time-defined movement across 
              the sky.
            
            • K - Koch: A variation of semi-arc division where 
              the ascendant's path is tracked back to the degree 
              it held when the Midheaven was at its current 
              position, dividing the corresponding oblique 
              ascension arc into equal parts.
            
            • T - Topocentric: A high-precision system mathe-
              matically similar to Placidus, but formulated 
              through a purely geometric and topocentric 
              framework. It calculates house cusps based on 
              the real rotational axis of the observer's 
              specific geographic location on the Earth's 
              surface rather than the Earth's center.

Philosophical and Technical Logics

    1) The Dorothean System (Hellenistic / Sect Logic)

    Dorotheus of Sidon (1st Century CE) based his system on the 
    concept of Sect (Hairesis), balancing the active elemental 
    quality with the diurnal/nocturnal nature of the planets. It 
    requires three rulers to show the beginning, middle, and end 
    of a specific area of life.

    • Fire: Belongs to the Diurnal sect. The Sun (prime diurnal 
      luminary) rules by day. Jupiter (diurnal benefic) rules by 
      night. Saturn (diurnal malefic) acts as the 
      stabilizer/participating ruler.

    • Earth: Belongs to the Nocturnal sect. Venus (nocturnal 
      benefic) rules by day. The Moon (prime nocturnal luminary) 
      rules by night. Mars (nocturnal malefic) acts as the 
      participating ruler.

    • Air: Belongs to the Diurnal sect. Saturn is placed here to 
      moderate its cold nature with the heat of the air signs by 
      day. Mercury rules by night, and Jupiter stabilizes.

    • Water: Belongs to the Nocturnal sect. Venus rules by day 
      (exalted in Pisces). Mars rules by night (domicile in 
      Scorpio). The Moon participates (domicile in Cancer).


    2) The Ptolemaic System (Aristotelian / Natural Philosophy 
       Logic)

    Claudius Ptolemy (2nd Century CE) simplified the Hellenistic 
    system. He rejected the third "participating" ruler because he 
    viewed triplicities through a binary Aristotelian physics 
    framework (Hot/Cold, Wet/Dry) rather than a symbolic lifetime 
    progression.

    • The Fire/Air Connection: Ptolemy grouped Fire and Air 
      together under the Diurnal sect. He assigned the Sun and 
      Jupiter to Fire, and Saturn and Mercury to Air.

    • The Earth/Water Connection: He grouped Earth and Water under 
      the Nocturnal sect. He assigned Venus and the Moon to Earth.

    • The Water Exception: For Water, Ptolemy introduced an 
      anomaly. Instead of giving it to a nocturnal planet, he 
      assigned it entirely to Mars (both day and night) to 
      counter-balance the extreme moisture of Water with Mars' 
      intense dryness. Venus (day) and the Moon (night) were 
      demoted to minor co-rulers.


    3) The Morinus System (Rationalist / Dignity Symmetry Logic)

    Jean-Baptiste Morin (17th Century) rejected both Hellenistic 
    sect and Ptolemaic physics. He sought a purely deductive, 
    symmetrical, and rational system based strictly on essential 
    dignities (Domicile and Exaltation) and the planetary nature.

    • Day Ruler (The Double Dignity Principle): The primary ruler 
      of a triplicity must be a planet that holds both a domicile 
      and an exaltation within that specific element.
      
          Sun rules Fire (Leo/Aries). Mercury rules Earth 
          (Virgo/Virgo). Saturn rules Air (Aquarius/Libra). 
          Jupiter rules Water (Sagittarius is hot/dry, but it 
          co-rules Pisces and is exalted in Cancer).
          
    • Night Ruler (The Cardinal Orientation Principle): The 
      secondary ruler is always the planetary ruler of the Cardinal 
      Sign of that specific element.
      
          Mars for Fire (Aries). Saturn for Earth (Capricorn). 
          Venus for Air (Libra). Moon for Water (Cancer).
          
    • Participating Ruler: The remaining major planet tied to that 
      element's nature is assigned to the final slot to complete the 
      mathematical triad.

• Syzygy Ante Nativitatem (SAN)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Syzygy Ante Nativitatem (abbreviated as SAN, and often called 
the Prenatal Syzygy) is a highly revered point in traditional and 
Hellenistic astrology. It refers to the exact degree of the last 
New Moon or Full Moon that occurred before your birth.

The SAN acts as your chart's cosmic seed or foundational 
blueprint—the literal atmospheric and spiritual gateway through 
which your soul entered the physical world.

1. How to Identify The SAN Type

The nature of your Prenatal Syzygy depends entirely on the lunar 
phase the person was born under:

    • Conjunctional SAN (Prenatal New Moon): If one was born 
      while the Moon was waxing (growing from New to Full), 
      his/her SAN is the preceding New Moon. This infuses the
      life with themes of initiation, fresh seeds, and forward 
      momentum. The person is naturally designed to launch new 
      projects and break new ground.
    
    • Preventional SAN (Prenatal Full Moon): If one was born 
      while the Moon was waning (shrinking from Full to New), 
      his/her SAN is the preceding Full Moon. This infuses 
      the life with themes of culmination, relational awareness, 
      and integration. The person is naturally wired to bring 
      things to completion and seek deep objective awareness.

2. Traditional and Modern Meanings

In traditional text analyses, the SAN carries massive weight 
across several core areas:

    • Vitality and the Hyleg: Ancient astrologers like Ptolemy 
      used the exact degree of the SAN to calculate the Hyleg 
      (the life-giving planet or point in a chart) to determine 
      a person's physical constitution, health baseline, and 
      overall life force.

    • The Soul's Karmic Imprint: The SAN marks the moment the 
      collective cosmic energy concentrated itself right before 
      the birth. The house and zodiac sign where the SAN lands 
      act as a "permanent sensitive point" in the chart. 
      Whenever a major outer planet transits this specific degree, 
      the person will experience massive, foundational life shifts.

    • The Path of Destiny: While the Vertex brings fated people 
      to the person, the SAN represents the purpose he/she is meant 
      to fulfill. It provides the background context for why  
      his/her soul chose your specific natal chart.

• The Moon's Temperament
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

In Classical Astrology (particularly within the Hellenistic and 
Medieval traditions), the Moon’s phases are foundational for 
calculating the native’s Temperament. 

This system determines your physical constitution, mental baseline, 
and psychological disposition by balancing the four primitive 
qualities (Hot, Wet, Cold, and Dry) which form the four bodily 
humors (Sanguine, Choleric, Melancholic, and Phlegmatic).

Because the Moon rules the physical body, the subconscious mind, 
and vital bodily fluctuations, the specific quadrant she occupies 
at the moment of your birth dictates your intrinsic physical and 
emotional baseline.

Here is how Classical Astrology attributes qualities and 
temperaments to each lunar quadrant:

1. First Quadrant: From New Moon to First Quarter

    • Primitive Qualities: Hot and Wet (Moist)
    
    • Associated Temperament: Sanguine
    
    • Characteristics: This phase corresponds to Spring. 
      It represents infancy, growth, and outward movement. 
      Individuals born during this quarter tend to be highly 
      social, optimistic, adaptable, and expressive. The 
      abundance of moisture grants immense psychological 
      flexibility, though it can sometimes lead to 
      restlessness or a lack of focus.
      
2. Second Quadrant: From First Quarter to Full Moon

    • Primitive Qualities: Hot and Dry
    
    • Associated Temperament: Choleric
    
    • Characteristics: This phase corresponds to Summer. 
      It represents youth, peak physical vitality, and direct 
      action. The heat stimulates outward drive, while the dryness 
      provides sharp focus and rigidity. Nativos born here are 
      often ambitious, passionate, competitive, and highly 
      motivated by challenges. They possess a powerful willpower 
      but must guard against impatience or irritability.
    
3. Third Quadrant: From Full Moon to Last Quarter

    • Primitive Qualities: Cold and Dry
    
    • Associated Temperament: Melancholic
    
    • Characteristics: This phase corresponds to Autumn. 
      It represents maturity, harvest, and deep introspection. 
      The heat recedes, leading to a highly analytical, objective, 
      and reserved posture. People born in this phase are 
      typically profound, serious, realistic, and highly 
      structured. There is a strong inclination toward the 
      intellect, organization, and a need for deep meaning, with 
      a tendency to process emotions internally.
    
4. Fourth Quadrant: From Last Quarter to the Next New Moon

    • Primitive Qualities: Cold and Wet (Moist)
    
    • Associated Temperament: Phlegmatic
    
    • Characteristics: This phase corresponds to Winter. It 
      represents elderhood, containment, dissolution, and 
      preparation for rebirth (especially the balsamic phase). 
      The cold slows down outer movement, while the moisture 
      seeks peaceful synthesis. Individuals born here tend to 
      be exceptionally calm, peace-loving, highly intuitive, 
      and receptive. They possess a quiet emotional resilience, 
      though they may occasionally struggle with inertia or 
      apathy.

•  Natal Temperament Module (Galenic Intensity Method)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This module calculates the native's humoral constitution using 
Claudius Galen's Scale of Intensities (2nd Century AD), later 
expanded by Medieval (Ibn Ezra) and Renaissance (William Lilly) 
astrological traditions.

Instead of a crude binary approach (where a planet is merely hot 
or cold), this engine adopts signed, weighted arithmetic 
magnitudes (-3 to +3) to map two polar axes:
    
    • Thermal Axis: Positive values indicate degrees of Heat; 
      negative values indicate Cold.
      
    • Moisture Axis: Positive values indicate degrees of Humidity; 
      negative values indicate Dryness.

Scale Calibration:

    1. Planets hold dynamic weights (Grades 1 to 3) as they are 
       the active agents of celestial light.
       
    2. Elements (Signs) act as a moderate environmental backdrop, 
       strictly calibrated at the 1st Galenic Grade (Magnitude ±1). 
       This prevents the sign's background qualities from 
       overwhelming or drowning out the essential humoral nature 
       of the planets positioned within them.

•  The Four Primitive Qualities (The Building Blocks)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

In Aristotelian physics, the universe is governed by the 
interplay of two active and two passive qualities. In the human 
body, they regulate metabolism, energy expenditure, 
and tissue structure:

    1. HEAT (Hot / Active): The principle of vital energy, 
       expansion, and metabolic acceleration. It drives the body 
       to disperse energy, speeds up the heart rate, and creates 
       an outgoing, active, and expressive demeanor.
       
    2. COLD (Cold / Passive): The principle of preservation, 
       contraction, and metabolic deceleration. It acts as a 
       cooling mechanism that conserves energy, slows down 
       physical processes, and leads to an introspective, 
       cautious, and receptive nature.
       
    3. MOISTURE (Moist / Passive): The principle of fluidity, 
       flexibility, and connectivity. It provides lubrication to 
       joints and tissues, makes the mind adaptable, and allows 
       the individual to absorb environmental influences easily.
       
    4. DRYNESS (Dry / Active): The principle of structure, 
       tension, and separation. It creates rigidity, definition, 
       and structural boundaries in the body. Mentally, 
       it manifests as focus, persistence, and resistance to 
       change.

This module evaluates the native's rational intellect, emotional

drawing from Ptolemy and Bonatti).

Unlike modern approaches, the traditional system analyzes the 
structure of the rational mind (the Intellect) and the irrational 
mind (the Emotional Soul) by tracking their natural significators, 
elemental dignity, and planetary governance.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. CORE ENGINE SIGNIFICATORS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The algorithm evaluates the condition of two foundational celestial
bodies:

  • ☿ MERCURY: Governs the rational mind, logic, speech, and 
               analytical skills.
               
  • ☽ THE MOON: Governs the irrational mind, subconscious, 
                memories, and habits.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SCORING & CONDITION MECHANICS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The final state of both significators is derived from their 
combined Essential Dignities (inherent virtuous quality) and 
Accidental Dignities (strength of material expression), which 
includes native handling of critical conditions like combust 
status.

  • HIGH ESSENTIAL & ACCIDENTAL STRENGTH:
    Intellect displays sharp, virtuous, and highly rational 
    cognitive patterns.
    
  • BALANCED INTELLECT:
    Displays functional, practical, and highly balanced everyday 
    logic.
    
  • NEGATIVE / DEBILITATED STATUS:
    Prone to erratic thoughts, anxiety, subjectivity, or mental 
    fog.

• INTROVERSION REFINEMENT [℞]:
  If Mercury is retrograde, the cognitive processor is naturally 
  inverted. 
  The native processes thoughts deeply inward, shifting into an 
  introspective, highly analytical but quiet mental state.

• EMOTIONAL SOUL FLUCTUATION:
  The stability of the Moon is further colored by the Lunar 
  Phase ID at birth:
  
  - New Moon: Internalized, deeply subjective, and instinctual.
  - Crescent Moon: Active, growing, and emotionally expressive.
  - Full Moon: Highly exposed, intense, and reactive.
  - Waning Moon: Reflective, detached, and deeply introspective.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. THE LORD OF THOUGHT (PRIMARY GOVERNOR)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The ultimate ruler of the native's psychological mindset is the 
"Almuten of the Mind", computed as a combined Almuten across the 
longitudes of both Mercury and the Moon. 

If multiple planets tie in essential points, the system applies a 
strict three-tier traditional tie-breaker cascade:
  1. Total Essential Dignities held over the key Hylegiacal Points.
  2. Total quantity of points governed throughout the chart 
     (Disposition).
  3. Total connectivity via planetary aspects (Chart integration).

PLANETARY GOVERNOR PROFILES:

  • ☉ THE SUN: Noble, clear, purpose-driven mind. Seeks honor and 
               leadership.
               
  • ☽ THE MOON: Highly intuitive, imaginative, adaptable, and

            debate.

               ethical judgment.
  
  • ♄ SATURN: Deep, silent, highly disciplined. Gifted for heavy 
              research.

  three traditional tie-breakers, the system flags a "Mixed 
  Influence", dynamically blending the core keywords and profiles 
  of the co-regents without visual formatting errors or structural 
  clipping.

• Part of Fortune
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Part of Fortune (or Pars Fortunae) is one of the most 
important Arabic Parts (or Lots) in astrology. It is a mathematical 
point in your birth chart calculated using the positions of the 
Sun, the Moon, and your Ascendant.

While your planets represent your psychological drives, the 
Part of Fortune represents a physical, tangible space where 
spiritual purpose, emotional alignment, and physical reality 
intersect.

1. The Core Meaning and Importance

In Classical Astrology, the Part of Fortune is known as the 
"Lot of the Body" and the "Lot of Prosperity."

    • The Gateway of Luck: It represents the area of life where 
      you experience the least resistance, where opportunities 
      flow naturally, and where you can achieve physical 
      well-being and material success.
    
    • The Integration of the Big Three: Because its formula 
      synthesizes the Ascendant (Body/Identity), the Sun 
      (Soul/Purpose), and the Moon (Mind/Emotions), the Part of 
      Fortune represents your optimal state of alignment. 
      When you live in harmony with your Sun and Moon, the Part 
      of Fortune "unlocks."
    
    • Materialization of Destiny: Unlike the Vertex 
      (which brings fated events), the Part of Fortune shows where 
      you can actively cultivate your own luck, joy, abundance, and 
      physical health.
      
2. The Calculation Matters (Day vs. Night Births)

In traditional astrology, the calculation changes based on whether 
you were born during the day or at night. This is crucial because 
using the wrong formula changes the entire meaning of your chart:

    • Day Birth (Sun above the horizon): Ascendant + Moon - Sun
    
    • Night Birth (Sun below the horizon): Ascendant + Sun - Moon
    
Note: Many modern online chart calculators default to the Day 
formula for everyone, which can completely misplace the Part of 
Fortune for night-born individuals.

3. How to Interpret It in Your Chart

To understand how your Part of Fortune operates, you must look at 
both its Zodiac Sign and its Astrological House:

    • The Zodiac Sign (The How): This shows the qualities you need 
      to cultivate to unlock your luck. For example, if it is in 
      Aries, your fortune comes through independence, courage, and 
      pioneering initiatives. If it is in Libra, abundance comes 
      through diplomacy, partnerships, and creating balance.
    
    • The Astrological House (The Where): This shows the arena of 
      life where prosperity manifests. For example, in the 2nd 
      House, it brings material wealth and financial stability. 
      In the 11th House, your luck comes through networks, 
      friendships, and community projects.
      
4. The Lord of Fortune

    • The Lord of Fortune: The planet that rules the sign where 
      your Part of Fortune lands is called the Lord of Fortune. 
      The condition, strength, and house placement of that 
      specific planet will tell you exactly how easily you can 
      access your abundance.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Feral Planet vs. Void of Course Planet 

In astrology, feral planets and void-of-course planets describe 
celestial bodies that are temporarily isolated, operating without
making connection angles (aspects) to other planets. While they 
share the trait of isolation, they function differently in natal 
charts versus daily transits.

🦁 1. Feral Planet 

A planet is considered feral (derived from the word "wild" or 
"beast") when it is positioned in a Natal Chart without forming 
any major aspects (such as conjunctions, squares, trines, or 
oppositions) to other planets.

    • Behavior: It operates in a raw, unfiltered, and untamed 
      manner. Because no other planets "talk" to it or moderate 
      its energy, it expresses its traits in extreme or 
      unpredictable ways.
      
    • Example: A feral Mars can manifest as immense courage or 
      sudden, explosive anger, as no other planetary force is 
      there to put a brake on its impulses.
      

🌌 2. Void of Course Planet (VoC)

A planet becomes void of course (or out of course) during daily 
celestial transits. This occurs when it has made its final major 
aspect in its current zodiac sign and travels toward the next 
sign without making contact with any other planet. 
This happens most frequently to the Moon (Void-of-Course Moon), 
but can apply to any planet.

    • Behavior: It represents a temporary loss of direction, 
      focus, or predictability. In horary or elective astrology, 
      actions taken during this time often lead to unexpected 
      outcomes, or indicate that "nothing will come of the 
      matter."
    
    • Example: Launching a business project while Mercury or the 
      Moon is void of course often results in delays, sudden 
      changes of plan, or a general lack of results.
      
      
•  Technical Definition: Applying Aspects

   A planet is technically considered Void of Course (VoC) when it 
   forms no further APPLYING major aspects before leaving its 
   current zodiac sign. 

    • Applying Aspects represent future events and dynamics that 
      are moving toward exact mathematical perfection.
      
    • Separating Aspects represent energies or events that have 
      already peaked and are fading away.

Therefore, a planet goes Void of Course because it has left its 
last separating aspect behind and has zero applying aspects ahead, 
meaning there is no immediate cosmic momentum to drive events 
forward.

• Part of Sickness, Accidents & Addictions (or Part of Misfortune)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What would be the description and the main points of the Arabic Part of Sickness Accidents & Addictions in Astrology?

The Arabic Part of Sickness (sometimes linked with trials, bodily vulnerabilities, accidents, or chronic habits) is a calculated sensitive point in a birth chart derived from traditional malefic planets, typically using the formula Ascendant + Mars − Saturn.

Description:

In traditional and medieval astrology, Arabic Parts (or Lots) are mathematical points projected onto the zodiac rather than physical celestial bodies. The Part of Sickness isolates specific vulnerabilities concerning physical health, bodily decay, functional blockages, or stress-induced crises. When activated by transits or difficult planetary aspects, it highlights periods where physical or psychological endurance is severely tested.

Main Points:

The Formula: Computed as the Ascendant plus Mars minus Saturn (in standard daytime/nighttime variations depending on the source text). It leverages Mars (representing heat, sharp pain, inflammation, or sudden accidents) and Saturn (representing cold, chronic illness, limitation, or wasting conditions). 

Accidents and Crises: Hard transits (conjunctions or squares) from outer planets or active malefics to this specific degree can denote times of heightened proneness to physical injury, sudden mishaps, or acute surgical interventions.
Addictions and Compulsions: Modern psychological adaptations of traditional lots view points tied to deep systemic distress and Saturn-Mars combinations as indicators of self-destructive coping mechanisms, chronic escapism, or deeply ingrained behavioral loops (addictions).

House and Sign Placement: The zodiac sign and astrological house where the Part of Sickness falls reveal where a person experiences the most acute somatic or structural stress (e.g., the 6th house for daily health routines/work stress, or the 12th house for hidden undoing and confinement/substance struggles).

Secondary Nature: Astrologers treat this point as a nuanced focal marker rather than an isolated cause of fate; it must always be read in concert with the overall condition of Mars, Saturn, and the ruler of the 6th/12th houses in the primary natal chart. 

The Arabic Part of Sickness is fundamentally related to both houses, the 6th and the 12th, but it shifts its focus depending on whether you are looking at physical ailments or psychological addictions.

Traditional and modern astrologers separate its expression into distinct areas of life:

Why it relates to the 6th House (Sickness & Accidents)

The 6th house is the traditional house of illness, physical injury, daily stress, and bodily imbalances.

    Physical Manifestation: When the Part of Sickness reflects acute physical ailments, sudden accidents, or inflammation (the Mars element), it manifests as a 6th-house issue.
    
    Daily Routines: It highlights how your daily work, diet, and stress management directly impact your physical well-being.
    
    Functional Illness: It points to the mechanical and biological "wear and tear" on the physical body.

Why it relates to the 12th House (Addictions & Hidden Vulnerabilities)

The 12th house is the traditional house of self-undoing, isolation, institutions, and hidden enemies (including self-destructive habits).

    Psychological Manifestation: When dealing with addictions, escapism, and compulsive behaviors, the Part of Sickness operates heavily through 12th-house themes.
    
    Chronic Cycles: It reflects the Saturnian element of the formula—chronic, deeply rooted psychological loops, mental confinement, and coping mechanisms that happen out of public sight.
    
    Institutionalization: Severe addictions or major accidents often lead to 12th-house outcomes, such as hospitalization, rehabilitation, or forced confinement.

• Part of Faith
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

And what about the Part of Faith? Is it related to religion only?

The short answer is no, the Part of Faith (traditionally called the Lot of Piety or Religion) is not related to dogma or formal religion alone.

In ancient Hellenistic and medieval astrology, "faith" (pistis in Greek) meant something much broader than subscribing to a church, temple, or specific religious doctrine. It represents your core worldview, your moral compass, what you place your trust in, and how you make sense of the universe.

1. What does the Part of Faith actually represent?

While it certainly dictates how a person might approach God or the divine, astrologers use this point to analyze several non-religious facets of a person's life:

    The "Internal GPS": It shows your capacity for hope, optimism, and inner conviction when things go wrong. It is what keeps you moving forward when there is no physical proof that things will get better. 
    
    Philosophy and Ethics: It dictates your personal code of ethics. An atheist or agnostic has a "Part of Faith" just as much as a devout priest; for the secular person, this point might show their deep faith in human nature, science, logic, or social justice. 
    
    Truth-Seeking: It tracks your relationship with absolute truth. It highlights how you separate truth from illusion, and whether you lean toward blind belief, deep philosophical inquiry, or total skepticism. 

2. The Formula Explains its Meaning

The traditional formula for the Lot of Faith/Piety heavily proves it is not just about blind religious obedience. It is calculated using the Moon and Mercury: 

    Day Charts: Ascendant + Mercury − Moon
    
    Night Charts: Ascendant + Moon − Mercury 

Why Mercury and the Moon?

In astrology, Mercury represents the rational mind, intellect, intellect-driven choices, and communication. The Moon represents your emotional body, your intuition, your soul's instinct, and your subconscious mind. 

By blending these two specific planets, the Part of Faith represents the exact intersection where your rational intellect (Mercury) meets your emotional intuition (the Moon). It is the sweet spot where your mind and heart agree on what is "true" and worth believing in. 

3. House Associations: The 9th House Connection

Because of its nature, the Part of Faith is deeply linked with the 9th House (the House of Purpose, Philosophy, and Long Journeys). 

    If it is placed well or makes good aspects to Jupiter (the planet of belief) or Mercury, a person easily finds a sense of higher purpose in life.
    
    If it is heavily afflicted by Saturn or Mars, the person might struggle with nihilism, deep existential crises, or feeling completely disconnected from any sense of meaning.
    
    
    
    
• Part of Lust
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

I would like to know about the Part of Lust, which is the part of pleasures and delight, right? Jupiter is the significator of this part, but I can't understand why this part is not formed by Venus, but Saturn instead.

The Part of Lust (traditionally known in medieval texts like Al-Biruni's as the Part of Delights, Pleasures, or Luxury) is computed using a formula that often confuses modern students: Ascendant + Jupiter − Saturn (not inverted at night, but depending on the tradition). 

Your confusion is completely justified! If Venus is the ultimate goddess of love, sex, and aesthetics, why does she seem entirely missing from this calculation? Why are the two cosmic opposites—Jupiter (the expander) and Saturn (the constrainer)—the ones building this point?

The explanation lies in how ancient and medieval astrologers defined the difference between attraction (Venus) and indulgence (Jupiter/Saturn).

1. Jupiter is the True Significator of "Abundance and Excess"

While Venus represents romance, connection, and what we find beautiful, Jupiter represents the physical act of consumption, magnification, and absolute indulgence. 

    Venus is "I like this; this is pleasing to my senses."
    
    Jupiter is "I want more of this; let us feast, overflow, and push the boundaries of pleasure." 

In traditional astrology, the Part of Lust is not just about having a sweet crush or holding hands. It tracks luxury, carnal satisfaction, and how we over-indulge our senses. Because Jupiter is the planet of growth and limitless horizons, it is the natural engine used to signify the capacity for immense delight and saturation. 

2. Why Saturn is Used Instead of Venus

To understand why Saturn is in the formula, you have to look at how Arabic Parts work. They are mathematical arcs of distance. The formula measures the distance from Saturn to Jupiter, and then projects that same distance from the Ascendant. 
This creates a cosmic tug-of-war that perfectly defines the psychological nature of lust and pleasure:

    The Spectrum of Self-Control: Saturn represents boundaries, restriction, denial, and discipline. Jupiter represents the complete removal of boundaries, lawlessness, freedom, and total indulgence. 
    
    The "Brakes vs. Gas" Dynamic: Measuring the distance between Saturn and Jupiter is literally measuring how far a person is willing to stray from discipline (Saturn) to pursue raw pleasure (Jupiter). 
    
    The Nature of Lust: True lust or extreme delight cannot mathematically exist without a boundary to push against. If there were no rules, societal taboos, or physical limitations (Saturn), the overwhelming thrill of breaking those boundaries for pleasure (Jupiter) wouldn't exist. 

Therefore, the Part of Lust measures how much your desire for expansion outruns your internal sense of restriction.

3. Where is Venus then?

Venus actually has her own separate Arabic Part in the traditional system, called the Lot of Eros (or Part of Love/Desire). 

The Lot of Eros is usually calculated as Ascendant + Venus − Spirit (with Spirit representing your conscious mind and willpower).

• The 7 Main Hermetic Lots
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

As you mencioned Venus and its Lot of Eros, can you explain me a little of the 7 main lots, the lots of the 7 traditional planets?

In Hellenistic and traditional astrology, the 7 Hermetic Lots (or the Lots of the 7 Traditional Planets) form a foundational system of calculating sensitive points. Introduced primarily by the astrologer Paulus Alexandrinus and later popularized by Firmicus Maternus, these lots are associated with the unique signatures of the seven traditional visible planets.

They represent different facets of the soul, fate, mind, and worldly experiences. Here is the breakdown of the 7 Main Lots and how they operate.


The 7 Main Hermetic Lots


1. Lot of Fortune (The Moon)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Core Meaning: Physical body, material prosperity, health, and chance.
    
    Explanation: It represents everything that happens to you that is outside of your direct control (accidents, windfalls, physical inheritance). It is deeply tied to your physical vessel and how you fare in the material world.
    
    Day Formula: Ascendant + Moon − Sun
    
    Night Formula: Ascendant + Sun − Moon
    
    
    
    
2. Lot of Spirit / Daemon (The Sun)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Core Meaning: Willpower, intellect, soul's purpose, and deliberate choice.
    
    Explanation: While Fortune is what happens to you, Spirit is what you choose to do. It represents your mind, your philosophy, your career drive, and actions driven by your conscious intent.
    
    Day Formula: Ascendant + Sun − Moon
    
    Night Formula: Ascendant + Moon − Sun
    
    
    
    
3. Lot of Eros / Love (Venus)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Core Meaning: Desire, attraction, affinity, and choosing what brings pleasure.
    
    Explanation: As mentioned earlier, this lot represents romantic attraction, deep friendships, artistic tastes, and what your heart instinctively reaches for. It signifies how you form harmonious bonds.
    
    Day Formula: Ascendant + Venus − Lot of Spirit
    
    Night Formula: Ascendant + Lot of Spirit − Venus
    
    
    
    
4. Lot of Necessity (Mercury)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Core Meaning: Constraints, legal battles, political struggles, and forced choices.
    
    Explanation: This lot represents the harsh realities of the material world where your choices are restricted. It governs enemies, litigation, contracts, and situations where you are forced to use your wits (Mercury) to escape a trap.
    
    Day Formula: Ascendant + Lot of Fortune − Mercury
    
    Night Formula: Ascendant + Mercury − Lot of Fortune
    
    
    
    
5. Lot of Courage / Audacity or Lot of Valor & Bravery (Mars)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Core Meaning: Bravery, aggression, risk-taking, and raw physical energy.
    
    Explanation: This point shows where you are willing to fight, take massive gambles, or defend yourself. It rules sudden outbursts of energy, military actions, and how you handle direct conflict or danger.
    
    Day Formula: Ascendant + Lot of Fortune − Mars
    
    Night Formula: Ascendant + Mars − Lot of Fortune




6. Lot of Victory (Jupiter)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Core Meaning: Success, assistance, justice, honors, and divine protection.
    
    Explanation: This lot marks the area of life where things flow easily and where you achieve triumph. It represents rewards, legal victories, good fellowship, and where you receive "grace" or lucky breakthroughs.
    
    Day Formula: Ascendant + Jupiter − Lot of Spirit
    
    Night Formula: Ascendant + Lot of Spirit − Jupiter
    
    
    

7. Lot of Nemesis (Saturn)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Core Meaning: Fate, ultimate justice, hidden blockages, and grief.
    
    Explanation: Associated with Saturn, Nemesis represents the cosmic balancing scale. It often reveals areas of chronic delay, hidden obstacles, old age, losses, or where you must face the consequences of past actions (karma).
    
    Day Formula: Ascendant + Lot of Fortune − Saturn
    
    Night Formula: Ascendant + Saturn − Lot of Fortune
    
    
    
    
How to Scan Them in a Chart?
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When astrologers look at these 7 lots, they use a quick hierarchy to determine which ones are screaming for attention:

    The Ruler: What planet rules the sign the Lot lands in? If the Lot of Victory is in Aries, Mars will tell you how that victory is won.
    
    The House: A Lot of Eros in the 10th house might mean finding your greatest desires or romance through your career, while in the 12th it might mean secret or hidden affairs.
    
    Planetary Aspects: Any planet sitting right on top of (conjunction) or squaring a Lot will deeply color how that lot manifests in your life.

• Arabic Parts in the Solar Revolution Chart
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 

In traditional predictive astrology (specifically following the Perso-Arabic masters like Abu Ma'shar), when analyzing a Solar Revolution (Solar Return) chart, you must absolutely consider both. 

However, they are used for completely different reasons. You do not just pick one; you track how the new, recalculated parts activate or interact with the original, natal positions.

1. Recalculated vs. Original: The Golden Rule

You MUST calculate the NEW positions of the Arabic Parts using the planetary and Ascendant coordinates of the Solar Revolution chart itself. These are called the Revolutionary Lots. They show what themes are actively being manufactured by the universe for that specific year.You MUST keep the ORIGINAL positions of your natal parts in mind. The Natal Lots represent your permanent, lifelong blueprint.

The magic of Solar Revolution analysis happens when you overlay the two charts (synastry style) to see how they collide.

2. How to Work with the Parts in Solar Revolution Analysis

Traditional astrologers use a strict hierarchical checklist to read the Lots in an annual chart:

Step A: Look for "Conjunction by Return" (The New hitting the Old)Check if a newly calculated Revolution Part falls exactly on top of a natal planet or a natal house cusp.

Example: If your newly calculated SR Part of Sickness falls exactly on your Natal Ascendant or your Natal Moon, that is a massive red flag that your physical health or vitality will be a primary trial during that specific year.

Step B: Cross-Check with Annual Profections

Annual Profection is a traditional technique where one zodiac sign/house becomes the "Time Lord" or focus for the year.

Look at whichever parts fall into the Profected Sign of the Year in both the natal chart and the Solar Return. If your Year is ruled by the 10th house (career), and the SR Lot of Victory lands right in that profected sign, a major professional breakthrough or promotion is highly likely.

Step C: Analyze the Ruler of the New PartOnce you calculate a new part in the Solar Revolution, look at the planet that rules that sign in the Solar Return chart.

If you calculate the new SR Part of Lust and it lands in Aries, look at Mars in the Solar Return chart. If Mars is in the SR 5th house making a beautiful aspect, the year will be heavily focused on indulging in pleasures and romance. If Mars is severely afflicted in the SR 12th house, that lust or over-indulgence might lead to hidden crises or self-undoing. 

Step D: Track Transiting Planets Over Natal PartsLook at where the actual Solar Return planets are sitting relative to your static Natal Parts.If Saturn in the Solar Return chart is sitting exactly on top of your Natal Lot of Eros, your romantic life or capacity for pleasure may feel heavily restricted, cold, or deeply dutiful for those 12 months.
